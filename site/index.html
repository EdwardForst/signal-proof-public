How it works

New signals disclose an hourly UTC interval, buy/sell direction and transaction quantity. Exact source times and prices are excluded. After each hour closes, signals are hashed with random nonces, linked in a hash chain, signed with Ed25519 and independently timestamped by an RFC 3161 TSA. Commitments are published before their contents are disclosed, 168 hours after the hour closes.

The signed boundary is the end of the signal hour; the TSA time records proof issuance. Timestamps establish existence by issuance, rather than at an exact moment within the hour. Legacy proofs retain their original format and release rules.
